Pork Carnitas (Mexican Pulled Pork)

Mexico

Cultural Background

Carnitas, meaning "little meats," is a cherished culinary tradition originating from the state of Michoacán in western Mexico. Traditionally made by slow-cooking pork in lard in large copper cauldrons (cazos) until tender and juicy, the meat is then crisp-fried in its own fat. This technique creates a delicious contrast of ultra-tender, succulent pork with golden, crispy edges that make it the ultimate filling for tacos, burritos, and tortas.


Did You Know?

Did you know that adding fresh orange juice and lime juice to the slow-cooking liquid tenderizes the meat while adding a subtle sweetness that caramelizes perfectly when crisping the pork in the oven or skillet?

Ingredients

Carnitas Pork & Rub

  • 2 kg / 4.4 lbs Pork Shoulder (Boston Butt), skinless, bone-in or boneless
  • 2 tsp Dried Oregano
  • 2 tsp Ground Cumin
  • 1 tbsp Salt
  • 1 tsp Black Pepper
  • 1 tbsp Olive Oil

Braising Liquid

  • 1 Onion (diced)
  • 1 Jalapeño (deseeded and chopped)
  • 4 cloves Garlic (minced)
  • 3/4 cup Fresh Orange Juice
  • 2 tbsp Lime Juice

Serving Options

  • Warm Corn Tortillas
  • Chopped Fresh Cilantro
  • Diced Red or White Onion
  • Lime Wedges
  • Guacamole or Avocado Slices
  • Salsa Verde or Pico de Gallo

How to Prepare

  1. Spice the Pork: Mix dried oregano, ground cumin, salt, and black pepper. Rub the spice mixture all over the pork shoulder.
  2. Combine in Cooker: Place the diced onion, minced garlic, and chopped jalapeño in the bottom of a slow cooker (or Dutch oven). Place the seasoned pork shoulder on top, then pour fresh orange juice and lime juice over the pork.
  3. Slow Cook: Cover and cook on Low for 8 to 10 hours or High for 5 to 6 hours until the pork is tender enough to fall apart easily with two forks.
  4. Shred the Meat: Remove the pork from the slow cooker onto a tray and shred using two forks. Reserve the savory cooking juices left in the cooker.
  5. Crisp to Perfection: Spread shredded pork onto a baking sheet, drizzle with some of the cooking juices, and broil in a preheated oven for 5 to 10 minutes until the edges are crispy and golden brown.
  6. Assemble & Serve: Toss the crispy pork with a few more spoonfuls of juices and serve hot in warm corn tortillas topped with fresh cilantro, diced onions, guacamole, and a squeeze of lime juice.
