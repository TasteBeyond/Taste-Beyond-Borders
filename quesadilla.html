Chicken Quesadilla

Mexico

Cultural Background

Quesadillas originated in central and northern Mexico, evolving from colonial-era corn tortillas stuffed with cheese into a versatile Mexican staple. Over time, flour tortillas became popular in northern Mexico and Southwestern Tex-Mex cuisine, paired with seasoned meats like grilled or shredded chicken and melted shredded cheese to create a crisp, comforting, handheld dish.


Did You Know?

Did you know that lightly toasting the tortillas in butter or oil before filling them helps seal in the juices, keeping the exterior crisp while the cheese melts evenly inside?

the perfect meal in minutes!

Ingredients

Chicken & Filling

  • 2 cups Cooked Chicken (shredded or diced)
  • 1 tbsp Olive Oil or Butter
  • 1 tsp Taco Seasoning or Chili Powder
  • 1/2 tsp Cumin & Garlic Powder
  • 1/2 cup Bell Peppers (diced)
  • 1/4 cup Red Onion (diced)
  • 1 1/2 cups Mexican Blend Cheese or Cheddar & Monterey Jack (shredded)
  • 4 Large Flour Tortillas

For Serving

  • Salsa or Pico de Gallo
  • Sour Cream
  • Guacamole

How to Prepare

  1. Season Chicken: Toss shredded cooked chicken with taco seasoning, cumin, garlic powder, diced bell peppers, and red onions in a skillet over medium heat until warmed through and fragrant.
  2. Warm Tortillas: Lightly coat a skillet with oil or butter over medium heat. Place a flour tortilla flat in the pan to warm slightly.
  3. Layer Fillings: Sprinkle a generous layer of shredded cheese over half of the tortilla, add a layer of the seasoned chicken mixture, and top with another layer of cheese.
  4. Fold & Toast: Fold the empty half of the tortilla over the filling to create a half-moon shape. Press down gently with a spatula.
  5. Cook Until Golden: Cook for 2 to 3 minutes per side until the tortilla is golden brown, crispy, and the cheese is fully melted.
  6. Slice & Serve: Remove from heat, cut into wedges, and serve hot with sour cream, salsa, and guacamole.
