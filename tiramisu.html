Classic Homemade Tiramisu

Italy

Cultural Background

Tiramisu is an iconic Italian dessert originating from the Veneto region of Italy in the late 1960s. Its name literally translates to "pick me up" or "cheer me up," referring to the instant energy boost provided by espresso and sugar. It quickly became one of Italy's most celebrated dessert exports worldwide.


Did You Know?

Did you know that quick dipping is the secret to perfect Tiramisu? Ladyfinger biscuits absorb liquid extremely fast, so dipping them into espresso for only 1 to 2 seconds per side prevents them from becoming soggy!

Comments


Coffee-soaked ladyfingers and rich mascarpone cream—
an irresistible classic Italian dessert!

Ingredients

Coffee Dip

  • 1 1/2 cups Strong Espresso or Brewed Coffee (room temperature)
  • 2 tbsp Sugar
  • 2 tbsp Coffee Liqueur or Rum (optional)
  • 24–30 Savoiardi (Ladyfinger biscuits)

Cream Filling

  • 4 Egg Yolks
  • 1/2 cup Sugar
  • 230g (8 oz) Mascarpone Cheese (softened)
  • 1 cup Heavy Cream (chilled)
  • 1 tsp Vanilla Extract

Topping

  • 2 tbsp Unsweetened Cocoa Powder (for dusting)

How to Prepare

  1. Prepare Coffee Dip: Whisk espresso, 2 tbsp sugar, and coffee liqueur (if using) in a shallow dish until sugar dissolves. Set aside to cool.
  2. Make Egg Yolks Cream: Whisk egg yolks and 1/2 cup sugar over a double boiler (or in a bowl over simmering water) until pale and thickened. Remove from heat and gently fold in the mascarpone cheese until smooth.
  3. Whip Cream: In a separate bowl, whip heavy cream and vanilla extract until stiff peaks form. Fold the whipped cream into the mascarpone mixture.
  4. Layer Tiramisu: Quickly dip ladyfingers into the coffee mixture (1–2 seconds per side) and arrange in a single layer at the bottom of an 8x8 or 9x13-inch baking dish. Spread half of the mascarpone cream evenly over top. Repeat with a second layer of soaked ladyfingers and top with remaining cream.
  5. Chill & Dust: Cover and refrigerate for at least 4 to 6 hours (preferably overnight). Right before serving, dust generously with unsweetened cocoa powder.
