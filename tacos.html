Classic Beef Tacos

Mexico

Cultural Background

Tacos originated in Mexico centuries ago as a simple staple, with roots tracing back to indigenous Mexican silver miners using rolled tortillas filled with spiced meats. The "old school" hard-shell beef taco evolved through Tex-Mex cuisine in the early-to-mid 20th century, becoming a worldwide comfort food favorite known for its crispy corn shell, savory ground beef filling, fresh lettuce, tomatoes, and tangy sour cream.


Did You Know?

Did you know that simmered taco meat tastes even juicier when you add a touch of flour or cornstarch to the spice mixture? It binds the juices into a rich glaze that coats every bite of beef!

Ingredients

Taco Beef Filling

  • 500 g (1 lb) Lean Ground Beef
  • 1 tbsp Olive Oil or Vegetable Oil
  • 1/2 Onion (finely diced)
  • 2 cloves Garlic (minced)
  • 1 tbsp Chili Powder
  • 1 tsp Ground Cumin
  • 1 tsp Paprika
  • 1/2 tsp Onion Powder
  • 1/2 tsp Dried Oregano
  • 1/2 tsp Salt
  • 1 tbsp All-Purpose Flour (or cornstarch)
  • 1/2 cup Beef Broth or Water

Taco Assembly & Toppings

  • 10–12 Hard Taco Shells (or warm soft tortillas)
  • Shredded Iceberg Lettuce
  • Diced Fresh Tomatoes (or Pico de Gallo)
  • Shredded Cheddar or Monterey Jack Cheese
  • Sour Cream
  • Salsa or Hot Sauce

How to Prepare

  1. Sauté Aromatics: Heat olive oil in a skillet over medium-high heat. Add diced onion and garlic; cook until softened and fragrant.
  2. Brown the Beef: Add the ground beef to the skillet, breaking it up with a spoon, and cook until browned throughout. Drain excess fat if needed.
  3. Add Spices: Stir in chili powder, cumin, paprika, onion powder, oregano, salt, and flour until well incorporated with the meat.
  4. Simmer Sauce: Pour in the beef broth (or water). Bring to a simmer and cook for 3 to 5 minutes until the sauce thickens and coats the beef generously.
  5. Warm Shells: Heat taco shells in a preheated oven according to package instructions until warm and crisp.
  6. Assemble: Fill each taco shell with a scoop of seasoned beef, then layer with shredded cheese, crisp iceberg lettuce, diced tomatoes, sour cream, and salsa before serving immediately.
