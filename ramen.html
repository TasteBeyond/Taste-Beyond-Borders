Ramen (Shoyu)

Japan

Cultural Background

Shoyu (soy sauce) ramen is one of Japan's most classic and iconic ramen styles, originating in Tokyo during the early 20th century. Traditional shoyu ramen features a clear, savory broth made from chicken or pork stock combined with a soy sauce-based seasoning tare. This quick homemade version captures the rich, umami-packed flavor profile of authentic Japanese ramen using simple pantry staples and instant techniques.


Did You Know?

Did you know that adding a small spoonful of lard or chicken fat to the bowl is essential for authentic ramen flavor? The fat insulates the soup to keep it hot, adds a silky mouthfeel, and carries all the aromas of the ginger and soy sauce!

Ingredients

Ramen Base & Soup

  • 1 tbsp Soy Sauce (preferably Japanese)
  • 1 tsp Chicken Stock Powder (Chinese or Japanese)
  • 1 to 1.5 tsp Lard (or Schmaltz / Chicken Fat)
  • 1/4 tsp Grated Fresh Ginger
  • Sliced Green Onions (white part preferred)
  • 300 ml (approx. 1 1/4 cups) Boiling Water
  • 1 portion Fresh or Dried Ramen Noodles

Quick Ajitama (Ramen Egg)

  • 1 Large Egg
  • 1 tbsp Soy Sauce
  • 1 tbsp Sake
  • 1 tsp Sugar

Optional Toppings

  • Nori (Dried Seaweed)
  • Sliced Green Onion Tops

How to Prepare

  1. Prepare Quick Ajitama: Poke a small hole in the bottom of the egg shell [00:00:27]. Boil in water for 6 to 7 minutes for a runny yolk [00:00:31], then cool in an ice bath and peel [00:00:43].
  2. Glaze the Egg: In a small pan, combine 1 tbsp soy sauce, 1 tbsp sake, and 1 tsp sugar over low heat [00:00:49]. Once tiny bubbles form, add the peeled egg and roll continuously in the sauce for 4–5 minutes until coated and deep in color [00:01:00].
  3. Build Soup Base: Directly in your serving bowl, add 1 tbsp soy sauce, 1 tsp chicken stock powder, 1 to 1.5 tsp lard, grated ginger, and chopped green onions [00:02:02].
  4. Cook Noodles: Boil your choice of ramen noodles according to package instructions [00:02:35].
  5. Combine & Serve: Pour 300 ml of boiling hot water into the bowl over the ingredients and stir until fully dissolved [00:02:42]. Thoroughly strain the cooked noodles to remove excess water, add to the bowl, and top with sliced soft-boiled egg, green onions, and nori [00:02:49].
