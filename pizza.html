Pizza

Italy

Cultural Background

Pizza dough has its roots in ancient flatbreads served across the Mediterranean, but modern pizza dough as we know it was perfected in Naples, Italy during the 18th century. Traditional Neapolitan dough relies on simple fermentation of flour, water, yeast, and salt. Today, homemade artisan pizza dough allows home cooks to recreate bakery-quality, crisp yet chewy crusts directly from their home ovens.


Did You Know?

Did you know that letting your mixed shaggy dough rest for 10 minutes before kneading (autolyse) allows the flour to absorb the water naturally? This simple trick makes kneading significantly easier and develops a better crust structure!

Ingredients (Makes two 14-inch pizzas)

  • 4 cups Bread Flour (or All-Purpose Flour)
  • 1 1/3 cups Warm Water
  • 2 tsp Salt
  • 1 tsp Instant Yeast
  • 2 tsp Olive Oil

Suggested Toppings

  • Pizza Sauce or Marinara
  • Shredded Mozzarella Cheese
  • Fresh Basil or Pepperoni

How to Prepare

  1. Mix Liquid & Yeast: Add 1 1/3 cups warm water to a large mixing bowl, sprinkle in 1 tsp instant yeast, and give it a quick stir [00:00:51].
  2. Combine Ingredients: Add flour to the bowl, sprinkle salt on top of the flour (avoid direct contact with yeast), and pour in olive oil [00:01:00]. Mix with your hands or a fork until fully incorporated into a shaggy dough [00:01:11].
  3. Rest Dough: Cover the bowl and let the shaggy dough rest for 10 minutes to allow the flour to hydrate [00:01:27].
  4. Knead: Transfer dough to a countertop and knead by pressing with the heel of your hand, folding, and turning for 5 to 7 minutes until smooth and elastic [00:01:36].
  5. First Rise: Place in a lightly oiled bowl, cover, and let rise at room temperature for 90 minutes to 2 hours until doubled in size [00:01:51].
  6. Divide & Shape: Gently punch down the dough, divide into two equal portions, shape into smooth balls, and let rest for 1 hour [00:02:01].
  7. Stretch & Bake: Gently press and stretch the dough out into a 14-inch circle [00:02:25]. Top as desired and bake in a preheated 500°F (260°C) oven with a baking steel or stone for 7 to 10 minutes [00:02:35].
